# Cryptball: stronger specials, touch controls, and crypt audio

## Objective and constraints

Ship directly into the existing game for user playtesting. Default mobile play uses one movement touch, one action touch, and the existing special meter as the sole dedicated gameplay button. Retain keyboard, mouse, multiplayer, all three sports, existing scoring and contact rules. Preserve unrelated working-tree changes. Use three GPT-6.1 Sol implementation agents with separate ownership; parent integrates and verifies.

## Specials

Keep opening charge 12 live seconds and shared repeat cooldown 24 live seconds. No increased shot power, automatic goals, extra Volley contacts, collision phasing, or recharge during pauses. All simulation effects and second-stage state must survive checkpoints/reconnects deterministically. Offensive actions retain readable release tells. Values below are initial tuning; document necessary code-grounded adaptations.

* Charge: 9-tick tell, 21-tick rush at 2.2x run speed, 12-tick recovery. Fixed heading, stop after first body hit, increased displacement (not increased stagger), ordinary immunity rules. No shooting during rush/recovery. Loose ball contact may clear at capped ordinary pass speed, never add velocity or become a charged shot. No automatic Volley attack. Miss exposes recovery.
* Sidestep: 6-tick tell, ~14-tick directed/steerable cut at 2x speed, 4-tick recovery. Resist body-check stagger during the active cut only; keep collisions and ball interceptions. Preserve existing soccer possession through cut without granting new possession. No attack during cut; return promptly to normal action control.
* Focus: 6-tick tell plus 180-tick window. Full movement during charge/strike; responsive acceleration. Admit while charging without resetting/adding charge, but do not cancel an existing swing/recovery/stagger. Aim can change during charge, normal release locks remain. First legal reception gets controlled damping / forgiving Volley receive window, once per activation; no extra legal contact or automatic catch from afar.
* Snap: defensive lunge with ~35% greater defensive contact reach (visual matches), small early steering correction then fixed heading. On first legal ball contact brake and reduce recovery; on miss retain commitment/recovery. Ground clearance capped at ordinary pass speed, Volley controlled upward dig preserving legal contacts. No powered reflected shot, forced possession, or repeated contact.
* Flutter: first short 2.2x burst (~10 ticks), up to 48 ticks for a second B press to trigger a separately directed burst. No automatic second burst. Normal movement between bursts; attack/pass forfeits remaining burst, with normal action timing. No shooting during burst. Ordinary soccer sprint possession release remains. Bot logic must deliberately request second burst. HUD exposes second-burst availability.
* Sting: 6-tick tell plus 180-tick prime. A strike charged for at most 6 ticks becomes an ordinary uncharged-power shot with a 2-tick tell; this lets a short touch hold use the quick release without secretly increasing shot power. Longer charges retain normal tell and power with reduced recovery. Pass tell >=2 ticks, recovery approximately halved. Same rule for every input device. Attempt consumes, whiff included. Allow short ordinary-range input buffer if compatible with existing rules; never create auto-homing or extended reach. Visible priming and release feedback.

Each move needs unmistakable phase feedback and success feedback, respecting reduced motion and ball visibility. Do not make goalkeeper displacement + recovery generate unavoidable goals. Test close-range use and same-player Volley restrictions explicitly.

## Controls

### Mouse and keyboard

WASD/arrows move; mouse points aim. Left press/hold/release strikes/charges. Right click retains slide/dive toward cursor; middle click passes on press, once per click. Keep J/K/I/Shift/Space/Q/B and cancel keys. Middle click is an alternate, never required (trackpads). The READY card also activates with mouse. Prevent context menu/autoscroll only on play surface; release/cancel state on blur. Mouse action clicks update aim immediately even without preceding pointermove. Wheel scrolling gets no game action to avoid accidental resource spending. No possession-dependent mouse meanings. Existing games establish left primary/right secondary conventions (Minecraft official controls https://www.minecraft.net/article/minecraft-controls); this specific mapping preserves Cryptball muscle memory.

### Touch: production default, not separate prototype

Use a dedicated, testable pointer-role recognizer producing existing CommandFrame actions. Touch handling must not enter mouse handlers. No global finger-count gestures; HUD contacts are excluded. First court touch owns movement until release; second owns action. Additional contacts ignored safely. Movement remains live during action/special use. Use relative floating stick, deadzone about 8 CSS px and full speed around 48px with bounded following anchor; screen-relative movement converted through existing court projection. Show unobtrusive active-only stick/aim cues, safe areas, readable landscape mobile layout; permit portrait without broken layout. Mirror support if feasible without destabilizing existing preferences.

Second touch released within 160ms with <14px movement => pass once. Displacement >=32px within 160ms => dive once in flick direction. Hold crossing 160ms => strikePressed/strikeHeld; no authoritative charge before recognition. Once shooting, movement cannot become a dive; displacement from action anchor controls aim (retain facing until displaced). Release action finger => strikeReleased. Movement thumb lifting while charge held cancels; interrupted/all-lift input favors no accidental shot. No phantom pass on cancellation. Returning movement thumb to neutral permits stationary shots. Ordinary contact/admission rules remain authoritative.

Movement thumb quick lift/re-touch near previous anchor (<=250ms, <=40px) plus directional drag => one ordinary dash; avoid dash on initial touch and prevent repeat while held. Tap teammate selection region => switch before generic action recognition, only if unambiguously targeted; do not steal movement touches. Resolve exact hit-test affordance from current renderer. These less-common gestures need concise help.

Special card is an actual accessible button with >=44px target, pointer ownership isolated from court. Tap while moving activates with current movement/facing direction. Follow-up Flutter tap activates second burst with new direction. Reject unavailable presses without spending cooldown; show status. Input help adapts to actual touch vs mouse/keyboard use. Teach: Drag to move; tap with other thumb to pass; hold/release to shoot; flick to dive; tap READY for special. Preserve pause/settings UI and normal menu scrolling. Touch-action:none on gameplay only, pointer capture by ID, cancellation on pointercancel/lost capture/blur/orientation/lifecycle. Never synthesize a release shot on interruptions. Browsers: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Pointer_events ; gesture design: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/gestures .

## Audio

Author original synth music and effects with existing Score/AudioEngine/M2Audio, no paid external service required. Deliver reproducible listening WAV sample rendered from the authored score if supported by local runtime. New material generated in code is the runtime source, not a disconnected mockup.

32-bar haunted arcade funk around 144 BPM in C minor: memorable two-bar syncopated hook + two-bar answer; occasional chromatic F# tension resolving to G. Bars 1-8 hook, 9-16 answer/counterline, 17-24 sparse breakdown, 25-32 hook return and alternate ending. Hollow organ/pulse lead, plucked bass, woody noise percussion, metallic bell accents, restrained ghost pad. Vary fills every 4/8 bars and leave rests. Distinct short title loop with correct length (no 22-bar silence). Overtime adds percussion/counterline on musical boundary. Avoid resets on routine volume changes if engine supports it safely.

Special sounds: Charge low horn/rush/impact; Sidestep airy swish/light step; Focus glassy two-note cue; Snap clack/scrape/success clamp; Flutter separated wingbeats; Sting rising buzz/release crack. Map authoritative special events and snapshot phase transitions without replaying historic activation on reconnect. Impact palette distinguishes body thud, strike, stone board, metal post, save. Subtle deterministic/cosmetic variation, impact scaling where event data allows, bounded voices and priority for saves/specials/goals. Gentle music ducking around major cues if supported; preserve independent volume, mute, pause/resume, audio unlock and lifecycle. Sound conveys success vs miss; no exaggerated success cue without actual contact.
